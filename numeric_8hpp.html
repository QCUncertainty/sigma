template<typename U>
bool sigma::detail::is_integer_exponent ( const U & exp)

True if exp has no fractional part.

Always true for an integral U. Used by pow() (Affine, Taylor, TaylorModel) to decide whether a zero-containing or negative-valued domain can be raised to exp at all – a negative base raised to a fractional power is not a real number.
