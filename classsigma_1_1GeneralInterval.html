Detailed Description

template<typename ValueType>
class sigma::GeneralInterval< ValueType >

Models an interval whose bounds may be open.

Interval represents closed intervals only. GeneralInterval adds the openness of each bound on top of one, so the half-open \( \lbrack a, b) \) and \( (a, b\rbrack \) and the open \( (a, b) \) are representable too. Openness decides which values are members, and nothing else: the width, median, and radius are those of the closure, the closed Interval with the same bounds. An infinite bound is never a member, so it is always open.

An Interval converts implicitly, and losslessly, to a GeneralInterval with both bounds closed (save an infinite one), so arithmetic that mixes the two types promotes to GeneralInterval. The reverse conversion discards information and is spelled out with closure().

In-place division by another interval.

A divisor merely bounded by an open zero, such as \( (0, 1\rbrack \), does not contain zero and is divided by normally; the quotient is then unbounded on that side. A bound of the quotient is closed under the same rule as for multiplication, except that a zero bound is closed when the dividend contains zero.
